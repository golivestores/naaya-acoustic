Acoustic design · Engineering · Delivery

Integrated acoustic environments for architecture.

NAAYA partners with architects, developers and contractors to deliver complete acoustic solutions — from design and simulation through to manufacturing and on-site commissioning.

Acoustic criteria, planning and consultancy.

Material systems, fit-out and site support.

01Who we are

A specialist partner for sound-critical spaces.

NAAYA is a multidisciplinary acoustic engineering and interior solutions company serving international projects where sound performance, spatial quality and delivery reliability must work together.

We are not positioned as a traditional material supplier. Our work begins with understanding how a space will be used, defining acoustic targets, coordinating with architecture and MEP systems, engineering the right material solution, supporting construction, and validating the final result on site.

One team. One responsibility. Complete acoustic delivery from concept to commissioning.

02What we do

Integrated expertise across design, engineering and delivery.

Our service platform allows clients to work with one coordinated partner for acoustic consultancy, simulation, manufacturing, specialist construction support, furniture systems and performance verification.

01

Acoustic Consultancy

Performance criteria definition, room acoustic strategy, design standards and specialist project advisory services.

02

Noise & Vibration Control

Environmental, architectural and building services noise mitigation strategies for complex and high-performance environments.

03

Simulations, Analysis & Solutions

3D acoustic modelling, prediction, ray tracing, noise mapping and design optimisation using industry-standard simulation tools.

04

Tender Documentation Support

Tender-stage acoustic design coordination, technical specification development, material performance documentation, and construction bidding support for consultants, contractors and developers.

05

Material Engineering

Custom acoustic wall, ceiling and interior systems developed around measurable performance requirements and architectural integration.

06

Manufacturing

Factory-direct production, prototyping, quality control and international supply chain support ensuring consistent engineering quality and delivery reliability.

07

Interior Fit-Out

Specialist fully integrated FF&E and acoustic construction support, detailing, installation guidance and on-site coordination for architectural and performance environments.

08

Testing & Commissioning

On-site acoustic measurement, performance verification, system tuning and final acceptance testing to ensure compliance with design intent and international standards.

03Technical capability

Simulations & Analysis

We model and analyse acoustic performance before construction begins — reducing risk and giving architects, consultants and contractors the evidence to make better decisions early.

Workflows span room-acoustic modelling, ray tracing, reverberation analysis, environmental noise mapping, MEP noise review and sound-insulation assessment — delivered with industry-standard simulation tools:

Odeon CATT CadnaA INSUL SoundPLAN COMSOL Ansys Rhinoceros Grasshopper Dante Controller DIRAC Virtual.Lab
