Who we are

We shape how a space feels.

For over a decade, NAAYA has worked where acoustics meets architecture. What began in 2014 as a systematic acoustic-decoration specialist is now a full-chain design house — interior, acoustic and AV design through to construction.

Three teams — design, acoustic R&D and installation — carry every project from first concept to final commissioning. One partner, one accountable line, true turnkey delivery.

Our philosophy

Performance you hear. Beauty you see.

01 — The sound of it

Acoustics, engineered.

02 — The look of it

Aesthetics, uncompromised.

Material craft and architectural restraint. Surfaces that recede into the design — or quietly become its feature — but never read as a compromise made for performance.

How we deliver

From simulation to sign-off — we close the loop.

01

Simulation

ISO 354 targets

We model the room, predict RT60, and tune the panel mix to ISO 354 targets before anything is made.

02

Manufacturing

Our own facilities

Custom production in our own facilities — every panel built to the specification, not adapted from stock.

03

Installation

On-site, sequenced

On-site installation support, sequenced with the build so the acoustic intent survives construction.

04

Testing

Measured, not assumed

Post-install acoustic measurement against the original target — proof, not assumption.

05

Acceptance

RT60 report

Sign-off with a measured RT60 report in hand — the loop closed, the room delivered.
