Tested to BS EN ISO 354:2003 in a reverberation chamber over a 50 mm fibreglass cavity, reported as the practical absorption coefficient αp. Vega’s grooved face and 7.5% rear perforation absorb the mid and low frequencies most rooms struggle with.
