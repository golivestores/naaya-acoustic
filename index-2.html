◆ Who we serve

Eight roles. One project.

From concept to completion, NAAYA touches every voice in the built environment. Hover any role to see how.

01 · Property Owners

Brand-defining spaces commissioned with confidence — turnkey acoustic delivery, documented performance, and a single point of accountability from concept to handover.

02 · Architects

Defendable specs and ready-to-drop assets — ISO 354 absorption data, Revit / CAD library, RCP examples, and tender support that survives the design review.

03 · MEP Consultants

Cleanly coordinated interfaces — pre-cut reveals, integrated lighting / grille details, BIM-ready families, and clearance notes for plenum and service runs.

04 · Acoustic Consultants

Tested data, not estimates — third-party NRC published per model across 125 Hz – 4 kHz, plus Sabine-grade reports for your project's RT60 budget.

05 · Interior Designers

Performance that never breaks the visual language — 14 wood species, RAL-matched paint, custom veneer, fabric-wrap, and 22 CNC art patterns to choose from.

06 · Contractors

Things that fit on site, fast — cut-to-size delivery labeled per drawing, installer-rated mounting kits, and field tech support when the shop drawings don't match reality.

07 · Fit-out Partners

The last 10% without the last-minute fires — direct factory ship, regional warehousing, free replacement on transit damage, and emergency air-freight on tight schedules.

08 · Distributors

A real partnership, not just a wholesale list — co-branded sample books, sales training, qualified project leads, and exclusive territory protection.

◆ How we work

From brief to handover — three steps.

01

Brief

You share project drawings, function, and RT60 targets — or use our free calculator first. We respond with a feasibility note within 48 hours.

Deliverable Feasibility note · 48 hr turnaround · Free, no sign-up
02

Spec

Our acoustic team models the room, proposes a panel mix tuned to ISO 354 targets, and delivers a tender-ready specification with Revit / CAD assets.

Deliverable RT60 report · Panel-mix spec · Revit / CAD library · 5 working days
03

Build

Custom production, factory-direct shipping, on-site installation support, and post-install acoustic verification — closing the loop with measured RT60.

Deliverable Manufactured panels · Install support · ISO 354 verification report
